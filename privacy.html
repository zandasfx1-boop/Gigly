Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

1. Who we are

Lagos Gig (“we”, “us”) is a gig marketplace for Lagos, Nigeria, at lagos-gig.vercel.app. We are the data controller for personal data collected through the app. Contact: zandasfx1@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and profile photo from Google sign-in, or the name and email you enter if you sign up with email and password.
  • Profile data you add: username, headline, skills, location, photo link and bio.
  • Gig data: the jobs you post, including category, budget, location and description.
  • Messages: the messages and voice notes you send in chats, stored so both people can read or hear them.
  • Notification data: if you turn on message alerts, we store your browser’s push address so we can send them. Alerts can show a short preview of the message on your lock screen.
  • Usage data: basic technical data such as device type and logs needed to keep the service secure.

When you buy a monthly pass we keep a payment record: amount, date and a Paystack reference. Payment card details are handled by Paystack and are never stored by us.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and run your account and show your profile and gigs to other users.
  • To review and approve gigs and keep the platform safe from fraud and abuse.
  • To manage your free trial, subscription and verification status.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Legal basis

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) we process your data because it is needed to provide the service you signed up for, because you gave consent (for example, to receive notifications), to meet legal obligations, and for our legitimate interest in security and fraud prevention.

5. Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google we receive only your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to create and run your Lagos Gig account. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Payments

Monthly pass payments are processed by Paystack. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, its amount and reference. We do not receive or store your card or bank details.

7. Who we share data with

  • Other users: your profile (name, username, headline, skills, location, photo, bio) and gigs you post are visible to signed-in users.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Google (sign-in), browser push services (Google, Apple or Mozilla) that deliver notifications, and Paystack, which processes payments. They process data only to serve us.
  • Authorities: where the law requires it.

Some providers process data outside Nigeria. We use providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete or anonymise your data within a reasonable period, except records we must keep by law (for example payment records).

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, object to processing, withdraw consent, and receive a copy of your data. Email zandasfx1@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

10. Security

Data is protected by encryption in transit, access rules on our database, and restricted admin access. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will notify you and the NDPC as the law requires.

11. Children

Lagos Gig is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has signed up, contact us and we will remove the account.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app. Questions: zandasfx1@gmail.com.
