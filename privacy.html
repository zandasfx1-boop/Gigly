Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what Gigly collects, why, and the choices you have. Gigly is used worldwide.

What we collect

Account details: name, username, email, profile photo and country or city you provide. Content: gigs, comments, messages, reactions, saved gigs and reports. Verification: any information you submit to get a blue tick. Payments: handled by our payment provider; we receive confirmation and subscription status but never see your full card details. Technical data: device type, browser and basic usage logs for security and performance.

How we use it

To run Gigly, show your gigs and profile to others, send notifications you ask for, process the Monthly Pass and verification, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve the service. We do not sell your personal data.

Who sees what

Your name, photo, country, gigs and comments are visible to other users. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation and to admins if a message is reported.

Services we use

We use Supabase (database, login and storage), Vercel (hosting) and a payment provider for checkout. They process data for us under their own privacy terms and may store it outside your country.

Storage on your device

Gigly stores a login session and your settings, such as theme and preferences, in your browser. We do not use advertising trackers.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time at the email below, and we will remove it unless we must keep some of it by law or for fraud prevention.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, the Nigeria Data Protection Act or CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to certain uses. Contact us to use these rights.

Children

Gigly is not for anyone under 18. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Security

We use encrypted connections and access controls, but no service is completely secure.

Changes

We will post updates here with a new date.
